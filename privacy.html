03How we use it

  • To provide the service — create your account, save and sync your data, and show your timeline, streaks and patterns. (Performance of our contract with you.)
  • To power the AI features you request — generate a suggestion, an insight, or a list of tasks from a schedule image. (Contract.)
  • To keep Visionary secure — prevent spam, brute-force sign-in attempts and abuse, enforce usage limits, and investigate incidents. (Our legitimate interest in protecting users and the service.)
  • To improve Visionary — understand, in aggregate, which pages are used and how fast they load. (Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.)
  • To communicate with you — send account emails such as confirmation and password-reset links, and reply to your messages. (Contract and legitimate interest.)
  • To meet legal obligations — respond to lawful requests and enforce our Terms. (Legal obligation.)

We do not use your content for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

04AI features

Suggest, reflection insights and Scan schedule are optional. When you use one, the relevant data is sent from our server to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response, and the result is returned to you.

  • Only the data needed for that request is sent: your current tasks, up to 14 days of reflection summaries, or the single image you selected.
  • Images are resized and re-encoded in your browser before upload, which removes embedded metadata such as location. Visionary does not store the image after the scan finishes.
  • Anthropic processes this data as our service provider. Under its commercial terms, API data is not used to train its models, and it is retained only as long as its data retention policy allows.
  • AI output can be wrong. Treat it as a suggestion, not professional advice. Pattern cards on the Patterns page are calculated in your browser, not by AI.

07How long we keep data

  • Account and content — for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, it is removed from our active systems immediately and from backups within 30 days.
  • Security logs — up to 180 days, unless we need them longer to investigate a specific incident.
  • AI usage counters — up to 90 days.
  • Schedule images — not stored by Visionary after processing.
  • Emails with us — as long as needed to resolve your request.

08How we protect your data

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and browsers are instructed to use HTTPS only (HSTS). Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider. Database rules ensure every account can read and change only its own records. We limit request sizes and AI usage, restrict which websites can call our servers, block repeated sign-in attempts, and log security events.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law. To report a vulnerability, email us. Our security contact is also listed at /.well-known/security.txt.

09Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Access and export your data at any time from Account → Download my data.
  • Correct your data by editing it in the app.
  • Delete your account and all associated data from Account → Delete account, or by emailing us.
  • Withdraw consent to analytics with Cookie settings.
  • Object to or ask us to restrict certain processing.

California residents. Under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

EEA, UK and Swiss residents. You also have the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email louisl4764@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will respond within 30 days (45 days where California law allows) and may need to verify your identity first. If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision.

10Children

Visionary is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are between 13 and the age of majority where you live, you may use Visionary only with permission from a parent or guardian. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
